#  E-Commerce Business Analytics – Dashboard Walkthrough

I developed an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard to explore e-commerce business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and customer retention.

The project focuses on translating business questions into meaningful analysis and actionable insights rather than simply creating visualisations.

##  Dashboard Walkthrough

### 1. Executive Overview

**Business Questions:**
- How is the business performing in terms of sales, orders, and customers?
- How do sales trends change across months and quarters?
- What is the contribution of new versus existing customers?
- Which product categories contribute most to sales?


![Executive Overview Dashboard](Screenshots/Executive_Overview.png)

---

### 2. Customer Insights

**Business Questions:**
- Who are the highest and lowest purchasing customers?
- Which countries contribute the most and least to sales?
- How do customer purchasing patterns differ?
- Which customers contribute significantly to overall business revenue?


![Customer Insights Dashboard](Screenshots/Customer_Insights.png)

---

### 3. Product Performance

**Business Questions:**
- Which are the top 10 best-selling products?
- Which products have higher return rates?
- How do different product categories perform?
- Which products require further investigation?

![Product Performance Dashboard](Screenshots/Product_Performance.png)

---

### 4. Customer Retention & Cohort Analysis

**Business Questions:**
- Are customers making repeat purchases after their first delivered order?
- How does customer retention change across acquisition cohorts?
- At which month after the first purchase does retention weaken?
- Which customer cohorts demonstrate stronger repeat-purchase behaviour?


![Customer Retention Dashboard](Screenshots/Customer_Retention.png)

**Key Insights:**
- Identified 7,401 customers eligible for cohort analysis.
- Found 7,380 customers with at least one subsequent delivered purchase.
- Identified 21 customers without a subsequent delivered purchase.
- Calculated an observed repeat purchase rate of 99.72%.
- Developed a monthly cohort retention matrix to understand repeat-purchase behaviour over time.

> **Note:** The observed repeat purchase rate measures whether a customer made at least one subsequent delivered purchase during the available order history. It does not represent monthly retention.

---

##  My Key Learning

This project helped me understand that effective data analysis starts with business questions, not charts.

I worked on translating business requirements into analytical questions, building relationships between multiple tables, developing DAX measures, and designing interactive dashboards.

Most importantly, I learned how to connect different analytical perspectives:

| Dashboard | Business Question |
|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Are we performing well? |
| Customer Insights | Who are our customers? |
| Product Performance | What are our customers purchasing? |
| Customer Retention | Are our customers coming back? |

Together, these perspectives provide a more complete understanding of e-commerce business performance.

##  Tools & Techniques

- **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard development and data visualisation
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX:** Business measures, time intelligence, ranking, and cohort calculations
- **Data Modelling:** Relationships and filter context
- **Cohort Analysis:** Customer retention and repeat-purchase behaviour

##  Business Value

The dashboard helps stakeholders explore sales performance, understand customer purchasing behaviour, evaluate product contribution, and identify opportunities to improve repeat purchases and customer retention.
